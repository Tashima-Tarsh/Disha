# DISHA Trending Optimization And Launch Plan

## Executive Summary

Current public state, checked on 2026-07-10:

- Repository: `Tashima-Tarsh/Disha`
- Visibility: public
- Stars: 1
- Forks: 0
- Open issues: 0
- Discussions: disabled before launch cleanup
- License detection: `Other`
- CI: Product CI, CodeQL, Pages, Dependabot
- Strongest asset: a rare positioning wedge around constitutional technology, evidence-led AI, policy gates, and provenance.

Realistic trending probability is low without a coordinated launch because the repo has almost no baseline community activity. Probability improves meaningfully if the maintainer ships a clean release, enables Discussions, creates starter issues, posts a strong demo, and drives authentic engagement during a 24-48 hour window.

Self-audit: the project is interesting enough to deserve attention, but only if it is presented as an evidence-first governance tool, not as a vague AGI dashboard. The credible hook is restraint: every conclusion must show its evidence chain.

## Best Positioning

Primary narrative:

> DISHA is an open-source Constitutional Evidence Operating System for policy-gated, source-backed intelligence.

Ranked hooks:

1. "No claim without source. No action before policy. No output without evidence."
2. "An evidence-led operating system for governed agents."
3. "Open-source constitutional technology for citizen-state accountability."
4. "Policy-gated AI that treats provenance as a first-class runtime primitive."
5. "A workbench for turning public records into reviewable intelligence chains."

Avoid:

- "AGI national surveillance"
- "CIA/RAW-style dashboard"
- "quantum intelligence" unless tied to specific bounded simulation code
- unsupported government statistics
- claims that imply operational authority

## What Can Kill The Launch

- Broken quickstart
- CI failures
- License ambiguity
- Overclaiming "AGI" or government capability
- Fake dashboard data
- Unsupported legal or incident claims
- Spammy community posts
- No visible demo
- No fast maintainer response in the first 24 hours
