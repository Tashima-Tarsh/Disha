Method

The public method is not a marketing layer. It is the operating discipline behind the private product: source registration, mission framing, policy evaluation, evidence capture, and traceable action.

01

Source before signal

A claim has no product value unless its source, custody, update path, and verification state are visible.

02

Policy before action

Analysis routes through governed lenses so the system can distinguish public facts, controlled material, sensitive claims, and prohibited actions.

03

Evidence before output

The product records what was asked, what was examined, what was rejected, and what remains unresolved.
