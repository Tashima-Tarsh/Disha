## Book Thesis

The next frontier is not more generated text. The next frontier is provable intelligence.

DISHA should become the operating layer where:

- sources are registered,
- parsers produce claim-level provenance,
- models advise but do not invent,
- policy gates action,
- evidence chains prove decisions,
- dashboards show only what can be defended.

## Blocked Claims

The book must not claim:

- complete government datasets,
- official certification,
- complete 2012-2026 crime statistics,
- parsed CAG findings,
- district incident heat,
- legal authority,
- source-backed figures that are not yet parsed.
