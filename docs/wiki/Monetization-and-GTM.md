# Monetization And GTM

DISHA should not be sold as a generic AI assistant. It should be sold as evidence-governed intelligence infrastructure.

## First Buyer

Teams that produce high-stakes reports and need source-backed claims:

- civic research teams,
- audit and risk analysts,
- legal/governance analysts,
- public-policy teams,
- cyber governance teams,
- regulated enterprise risk teams.

## Pricing Ladder

### Pro

- personal workspace,
- bring-your-own OpenAI key,
- source probe dashboard,
- claim provenance export.

### Team

- shared workspaces,

- parser history,

- usage limits.

### Enterprise

- SSO/OIDC,
- tenant isolation,
- managed database,
- private deployment,
- custom parser packs,

- support and SLA.
